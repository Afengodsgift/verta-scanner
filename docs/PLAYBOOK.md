# VERTA first-revenue playbook (v0.1, hypotheses, adjust after 10 conversations)

## Who to sell to
WooCommerce / non-Shopify merchants in the US with real catalogs (50+ products), B2B or considered purchases (parts, equipment, supplies, wholesale): they have gated prices, spec PDFs and quote flows, which is where agents fail and where the audit shows value. Avoid tiny hobby shops (no budget) and big brands (no access).

## The ladder (one offer at a time)
1. Free scan (done): the hook. Never the product.
2. Agent audit, first 3 customers at a founder price (suggest $150-300, say it is a pilot): agent runs the missions on their store, report with screenshots + prioritized fixes + a 20-minute walkthrough.
3. Fix package (suggest $500-1,500 per store depending on platform): structured product data, price/availability markup, policy markup, retest with before/after score.
4. Later: monitoring subscription once 3+ merchants have asked "can you keep checking this?".
Price from what the first three actually say yes to, not from this file.

## This week
- Day 1: set `public/config.js` (email, booking link, your name) and push. Rescan the stores you tested; fix anything that still looks wrong.
- Day 1-2: use /prospect.html to scan 10 stores; keep the ones scoring under 65 with at least one critical fix you can explain in one sentence.
- Day 2-5: send 5 messages a day (email to a real person: owner, ecommerce manager). Use the generated email, but change the first line to something true about their store. Offer the free report, not a call.
- Day 3+: when someone replies, send the report link, offer the 20-minute walkthrough, then the audit.
- Log every reply and objection. After 10 conversations, change the offer wording, not the product.

## Before you sell the agent audit
- Run the agent (with --llm) on 3 real stores you have permission for, read the evidence yourself, and check every finding is true. A wrong finding in a paid report costs more than a missing one.
- Never run payment tests on a store that has not agreed in writing.
- Say plainly in every report what was not tested.

## Rules for outreach
Be specific, be honest about what the free scan can and cannot see, no fear claims, no revenue promises, stop when someone says no.
