How Verta scores a store

Verta looks at a store the way an AI shopping assistant would, then reports what it saw, why it matters and how to fix it.

What the scan does

Verta opens your homepage, robots.txt and sitemap, finds product pages, reads their structured data and visible text, looks for shipping and returns policies, and checks for signals of cart and checkout. It reads only public pages, with a clearly labelled visitor (VertaScanner).

The eight layers

DiscoverabilityCan agents find the store and its products?15

InteractionCan they move around without a human's eyes and mouse?10
Transaction readinessCould an authorized agent get from product to purchase?10

These weights are a working hypothesis, not an industry standard. They will change as Verta learns from real agent tests.

Reading the score

80 to 100Complete. Product data is in good shape. Any critical problem caps the label at Mostly complete.
60 to 79Mostly complete. Most foundations exist, with meaningful gaps.
40 to 59Needs fixes. Significant gaps can keep products out of Google Shopping and AI search.
0 to 39Hidden. Search engines and AI assistants can barely read the store.
Incomplete scanNo product page could be read, so only the storefront was scored.

"Couldn't tell" and "Not tested"

When the evidence is thin, for example a page built by scripts that the scanner cannot run, Verta says it couldn't tell instead of failing the check. Those checks are left out of the score. A layer shown as Not tested was not scored, which is different from scoring zero.

What Verta does not test

  • Checkout, payment and anything behind a login.
  • Pages built only by scripts the scanner cannot run.
  • How any particular AI assistant behaves today. Verta measures how readable and operable a store is, not a prediction.

The score is a working framework, not a certification.

Beyond the free scan

The free check reads pages. The full agent audit goes further: an AI agent runs real shopping missions on the store (find a product, read the price and stock, add to cart, reach checkout) and the report includes screenshots as evidence. Payment tests run only on stores that have agreed to them.
