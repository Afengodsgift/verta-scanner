Is your store ready for AI shopping agents?

People are starting to ask AI assistants what to buy. Verta shows you, with evidence, whether those assistants can find your products, read your prices and stock, and complete a purchase. Then we fix what is in the way.

The free check uses your public pages only. We never need access to your store.

  1. Step 1

    Tell us your store

    Send your store address. That is all we need for the first check.

    Fix it yourself with the instructions in the report, or have us do it and retest so you can see the before and after.

What Verta does

Free store check

We read your public pages the way a shopping assistant does and send you the readiness report. No cost, no access to your store.

Agent audit

An AI agent runs real shopping missions on your store: find a product, read the price and stock, add to cart, reach checkout. You get screenshots as proof and a prioritized fix list.

Fix and retest

We make the changes: structured product data, price and stock markup, policy information. Then we run the same missions again and show the score change.

What we look at

  • Can assistants find your store and products?
  • Can they understand what each product is?
  • Is your product data readable by machines?
  • Can they tell what is in stock?
  • Can they read price and currency?
  • Can they read shipping and returns rules?
  • Can they use your pages without a human?
  • Could an authorized agent reach a purchase?

Questions

Do you need access to my store?

No. The free check reads public pages only, like any visitor.

Will you place orders on my store?

No. Checkout is only observed up to the payment step. We run test payments only on stores that have agreed to it in writing, using processor test cards.

Is the score an official rating?

No. It is a working framework that measures how readable and usable a store is for AI assistants. It is not a certification and it does not predict sales.

What does it cost?

The free check is free. Agent audits and fixes are quoted after you have seen your report.

What can't the free check see?

Anything behind a login, checkout and payment, and pages that only appear after scripts run. The report says exactly what it could not test.
